About Matt Tabak

Matt Tabak is a script consultant based in Los Angeles. He is a former studio executive at Touchstone/Disney, Paramount and Warner Bros., a WGA screenwriter and director for 30 years, an attorney, and a lecturer at Chapman University's Dodge College of Film and Media Arts.

Studio executive career

As a studio executive, Matt developed more than 150 projects and read more than 2,000 scripts. He developed the script 3000, which became Pretty Woman, and coached Julia Roberts on her audition. He bought the screenplay for Face/Off after every other studio had passed, and developed the project. He discovered Andrew Kevin Walker, the writer of Se7en, and gave him his first screenwriting job.

He hired and worked with writers and directors including J.J. Abrams, Ang Lee, Tony Scott, Sidney Lumet, Brian Helgeland, Jeph Loeb, Chris Keyser and Jonathan Hensleigh. Years at each studio

Writing and directing

Matt has written more than 25 screenplays and pilots, across genres from romantic comedy to psychological thriller to historical epic. He adapted Dostoyevsky's The Brothers Karamazov for Francis Ford Coppola and Sony Pictures. He wrote and directed Auggie Rose, starring Jeff Goldblum and Anne Heche, his feature directorial debut, which led Entertainment Weekly to call him "the long-lost Coen Brother." The film premiered on Cinemax and was released on video as Beyond Suspicion.

Teaching

Matt is a lecturer in Film and Media Arts at Chapman University's Dodge College. He previously taught screenwriting at the University of San Francisco and has given pitching seminars across the country.

Law and business

Matt is a former Wall Street transactional attorney. He has advised writers, directors and producers on deal-making, contracts and career strategy.

Working with Matt

Matt offers screenplay notes, TV pilot notes, treatment analysis, logline punch-ups and career, deal and production advice. Every service includes a live conversation by Zoom or phone. See screenplay notes and rates.
