About Matt Tabak

Matt Tabak is a script consultant based in Los Angeles. He is a former studio executive at Touchstone/Disney, Paramount and Warner Bros., a WGA screenwriter and director for 30 years, an attorney, and a lecturer at Chapman University's Dodge College of Film and Media Arts.

Teaching

Matt is a lecturer in Film and Media Arts at Chapman University's Dodge College. He previously taught screenwriting at the University of San Francisco and has given pitching seminars across the country.

Law and business

Matt is a former Wall Street transactional attorney. He has advised writers, directors and producers on deal-making, contracts and career strategy.

Working with Matt

Matt offers screenplay notes, TV pilot notes, treatment analysis, logline punch-ups and career, deal and production advice. Every service includes a live conversation by Zoom or phone. See screenplay notes and rates.
