THE WHO:

This is the website for Matt Tabak, screenwriting consultant.

Matt is a writer/director/producer, a former studio executive, and an attorney.

He has worn many hats in the business; he's been a part of the studio system, the indie world, and the streaming universe.

Now for the first time he is offering his services to the public.

If you want script notes, help with production issues, or assistance with legal, financial or career concerns you have come to the right place.

THE BRAG:

Entertainment Weekly Called Matt “The long lost Coen Brother.”

Billy Bob Thornton: “Tabak gives some of the best notes in town.”

THE EXPERIENCE:

Currently, a Professor at Chapman University/Dodge Film School.

Matt worked for Touchstone/Disney, Paramount and Warner Bros, and Jerry Bruckheimer.

Developed the script 3000 which became “Pretty Woman” and coached Julia Roberts on her audition.

He developed over 150 projects. Read over 2000 scripts.

He hired and worked with hundreds of writers and directors including:

JJ Abrams, Ang Lee, Brian Helgeland (LA CONFIDENTAIL, MYSTIC RIVER), Jeph Loeb (DAREDEVIL, COMMANDO), Tony Scott, Chris Keyser (PARTY OF FIVE and Chief WGA Negotiator), Jonathon Hensleigh (ARMAGEDDON) and Sidney Lumet and many others.

Other career notables:

Discovered Andrew Kevin Walker (SEVEN) and gave him his first screenwriting job.

As a studio executive, gave such good notes on a project that Jeffrey Katzenberg (CHAIRMAN, WALT DISNEY and DREAMWORKS) wanted him to rewrite the script.

Bought the screenplay for “Face-Off” when every other studio had passed and developed the project.

Worked as a writer in all genres from rom-coms to psychological thrillers to historical epics and biopics.

Adapted Dostoyevsky's The Brothers Karamazov for Francis Coppola and Sony Pictures.

Has written over 25 screenplays and pilots.

Wrote and directed “Auggie Rose,” starring Jeff Goldblum and Anne Heche.

Recently wrote and produced “The Other Zoey” for Amazon, a number one rom-com hit.

Taught screenwriting at University of San Francisco. Given pitching seminars across the country.

Was a transactional attorney on Wall Street and has advised numerous writers, directors and producers on deal making, contracts and strategy.

WGA member for 30 years.
