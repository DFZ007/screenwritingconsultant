Better than coverage

Coverage is a quick synopsis and boilerplate notes from a reader you'll never meet. Tabak reads every page himself. No readers, no assistants, no AI. It's a one-on-one conversation where he walks you through exactly what isn't working and gives you real creative solutions.

Who's reading your script

Matt Tabak is a former studio executive at Touchstone/Disney, Paramount and Warner Bros. He developed Pretty Woman, bought and developed Face/Off, and has read more than 2,000 scripts. He has been a WGA screenwriter and director for 30 years, and is Professor of Screenwriting at Chapman University's Dodge College of Film and Media Arts. More about Matt.

Questions

Struggling to get your next draft to another level?

That's exactly what screenplay and TV pilot notes are for: a comprehensive reading of your draft, then a live call of up to two hours with detailed analysis, ideas, and concrete solutions on how to fix it.

Notes from friends and Reddit not elevating your material?

Friends are kind and strangers online are guessing. I read over 2,000 scripts as a studio executive and know what makes a buyer say yes, and as a working writer I know how to get the fix onto the page.

Do I get written notes?

Yes, for feature, TV pilot and treatment notes. In addition to the live call, you get a written summary of my notes and our call, so you have something to refer back to. The call is still the heart of it: story and script editing where we can dig into your questions in real time, and you're welcome to record it. For lengthier written notes or page notes, contact me for a quote.

Do you use AI to read scripts?

No. I read every page myself and don't use AI to read, summarize or evaluate your script. No readers, no assistants, no AI.

How long does it take?

It depends on the length of the material and my schedule. You'll get a firm date for your notes call when you book.

Can I just send you my script?

Please don't. Start with a short inquiry. If we're a fit, you'll book the service, and then you send your material.

Is my script kept confidential?

Yes. I don't share or pitch your material to anyone without your written permission, and you keep all rights to it.

Will you introduce me to agents, managers or producers?

No. I have plenty of industry relationships, but this service isn't about access. It's about story, character and craft. I don't pass scripts along, make introductions or promise representation, a sale or a meeting. What you get is a better script, and a better storyteller.
