Questions

Can you help with a contract?

Yes. As a former Wall Street transactional attorney, I've advised numerous writers, directors and producers on deal making and contracts. Legal, financial, production and career help is $200 an hour. It's business guidance from experience, not legal representation.

Have a question about a producer's offer?

Book an hour of legal, financial, production and career help. You'll get my read, as a former studio executive, on what the offer means, what's standard, and what to push on. $200 an hour.

Want help understanding the financial and legal side of the business?

I've seen the business from the studio, the writer's chair and the deal table. An hour of career help covers options, financing, packaging, representation and how deals actually get made.

Is contract help the same as legal representation?

No. I draw on my experience as a former Wall Street transactional attorney and studio executive, but this is business guidance, not legal representation, and it doesn't create an attorney-client relationship. For legal advice on your specific situation, hire a licensed entertainment attorney.

Is my script kept confidential?

Yes. I don't share or pitch your material to anyone without your written permission, and you keep all rights to it.
